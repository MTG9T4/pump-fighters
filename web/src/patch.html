PUMP FIGHTERS

for PSP and PPSSPP

Make the PUMP FIGHTERS SF33RD.AFS from your own copy of Street Fighter III 3rd Strike. Your file is checked and patched in this browser; nothing is uploaded.

  1. Pick your SF33RD.AFS

    PS2 US version (v1.0), the file in the disc's THIRD folder (642,492,416 bytes).

  2. Save the modded file

    DOWNLOAD SF33RD.AFS

  3. Put it on your PSP or in PPSSPP

    PUMP FIGHTERS runs on the 3s-psp port. Get its EBOOT.PBP from the 3s-psp project's own release (we can't share it), then lay the folder out like this:

    PSP/GAME/3rd-strike/
  EBOOT.PBP
  resources/
    SF33RD.AFS   ← the modded file

    In PPSSPP the PSP folder is in its memory stick folder (on Windows usually Documents/PPSSPP/PSP). Keep your original SF33RD.AFS somewhere else.
